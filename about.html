How It Started

Born on a Rainy Friday in Austin

In 2023, our founders tried to book two local taco and burger trucks for a 120-person company celebration. It took three weeks of dead phone numbers, buried social media DMs, and PDF menus before anything was confirmed.

We realized both sides deserved better: diners and planners needed one live directory with real hours and upfront minimums, while independent chefs needed a modern booking engine that filled their calendars without eating their margins.
